# Code of conduct

Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and unsafe handling of reported vulnerabilities are not acceptable.

Assume good intent while challenging claims with evidence. Security disagreements should focus on threat models, code, tests, and operational tradeoffs. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact channels. Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
